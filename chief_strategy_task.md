### GLEE Framework for the Business

1. **Get Big at - Integrated Video Solutions**
   - **Reason**: Given our existing capabilities and market trends, we should focus on obtaining a significant share of the video creation market by offering a seamless solution that integrates various media formats. This aligns with the rising demand for quick and efficient content creation.

2. **Lead - AI-Powered Content Creation**
   - **Reason**: To stay competitive, we must lead in AI-driven video editing and content personalization. As AI technologies continue to evolve, leading this aspect will enhance our attractiveness to users who seek innovative solutions and streamline their content creation processes.

3. **Expand - Real-time Analytics and Training Resources**
   - **Reason**: Since there is a gap in the market for tools that not only provide video solutions but also educate users on maximizing these tools, we should expand by offering analytics capabilities and comprehensive user training resources. This will improve user engagement and retention, allowing us to target a broader audience.

### GEM Ranking for the Business

1. **Monetization**: Focus on improving revenue conversion from leads to paying customers. The aim is to increase the lifetime value of each customer through tiered pricing models or premium features.
2. **Engagement**: Enhance user retention through improved onboarding and continuous customer support. Strategies to reduce churn should be prioritized heavily in our efforts.
3. **Growth**: While achieving stable growth in user acquisition, we need to stabilize new user signups and address the fluctuating growth rates observed in recent months.

### Proxy Metrics for GEM

- **Growth**: The measure for growth can be "Can drive a 10% increase in annual user signups year-on-year." This reflects the goal of stabilizing acquisition strategies and broadening our customer base.
  
- **Engagement**: The metric for engagement could be "Achieve a 30% Video Activation Rate for onboarded users." A focus on active usage of key features like video editing will indicate user satisfaction and value perception.
  
- **Monetization**: For monetization, a suitable metric would be "Increase the revenue conversion rate to 3% within the next year." This will help ensure that a greater proportion of visitors are converting into paying customers.

Aligning these strategies with the current market and user behaviors will enable our business to strategically drive growth, enhance engagement, and optimize monetization pathways effectively.
